Atrang Club is the vibrant heart of cultural activities at UEM Jaipur. We are a dynamic community of passionate students celebrating art, music, dance, drama, and all forms of creative expression. Founded with the vision to nurture talent and cultural diversity, our club provides a platform for students to showcase their abilities, learn new skills, and connect with fellow enthusiasts. Through workshops, performances, and competitions, we bring the campus alive with creativity and artistic excellence.

Our Vision

To foster creativity and cultural awareness among students, making UEM Jaipur a recognized hub of artistic excellence and cultural innovation.

Our Mission

To organize inspiring events, workshops, and competitions that encourage students to explore and express their cultural heritage while developing new skills.

Our Values

Creativity, Inclusivity, Collaboration, Excellence, and Cultural Appreciation guide everything we do at Atrang Club.
